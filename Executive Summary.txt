 Executive Summary
This document outlines the comprehensive plan for the network switch refresh project at the Amsterdam and Singapore colocation data centers. The primary goal is to replace aging Cisco Catalyst 3850 switch stacks with a modern, scalable Cisco Nexus-based architecture. This upgrade supports critical initiatives such as the SD-WAN transformation, Cisco DNA Center implementation, and ongoing firewall refresh efforts, while significantly expanding 10G+ port availability and improving overall network resiliency. Additionally, the project consolidates legacy top-of-rack (ToR) switches connected to VxRack/Dell infrastructure into the new access layer for streamlined operations.

Project Objectives

Retire Legacy Infrastructure: Decommission aging Cisco Catalyst 3850 switch stacks that are end-of-life and no longer aligned with long-term IT strategy.

Modernize Network Fabric: Introduce a scalable, high-performance Nexus-based architecture to support current and future enterprise workloads.

Standardize Global Design: Align the Amsterdam and Singapore data centers with a consistent topology for operational efficiency.

Enhance Redundancy and Performance: Implement vPC and multipath designs to ensure high availability and improved traffic handling.

Expand 10G/25G Capacity: Add significant SFP+/SFP28 port density to support:
◦ SD-WAN transformation
◦ Cisco DNA Center implementation
◦ Firewall refresh/upgrade projects

Upgrade Out-of-Band Infrastructure: Replace legacy Cisco terminal servers with Opengear 32-port console servers for improved remote access, monitoring, and resilience.

Consolidate ToR Infrastructure: Decommission legacy top-of-rack Nexus switches used for VxRack/Dell servers, and migrate functionality into the new access layer switches.

 

Current Infrastructure Overview
Legacy Switches

Model: Cisco Catalyst 3850

◦ Limited 10G port availability

◦ Reduced flexibility for segmentation, automation, and monitoring

Configuration: Layer 2 uplinks to 3850 stacks

Status: To be decommissioned and integrated into new Nexus access layer

Deployment Plan
Staging and Pre-Checks

Configure and test Nexus switches offsite

Validate port mapping and VLAN configurations

Perform config backups of legacy switches


Migration Strategy

Physical Installation
◦ Rack and cable new switches per rack elevation and cabling plans
◦ Connect management ports to Opengear devices

L2 Interconnection
◦ Create Layer 2 port-channels between legacy 3850s and Nexus access switches
◦ Validate STP topology, root bridge roles

L3 Cutover
◦ Move SVIs and default gateway responsibilities to Nexus core switches
◦ Update dynamic routing peers (OSPF/BGP)

Cable Migration
◦ Incrementally migrate connected devices to Nexus switches
◦ Migrate VxRack/Dell server connections from legacy ToR switches
◦ Verify connectivity, VLAN, and throughput per move

Decommissioning
◦ Remove legacy 3850 stacks and ToR Nexus switches
◦ Update asset inventory and dispose of hardware per company policy
Post-Migration Validation

Conduct reachability tests and throughput benchmarks

Confirm routing tables and VLAN segmentation

Monitor for performance or error anomalies
